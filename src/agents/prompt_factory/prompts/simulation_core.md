## Simulation Core

- Resolve player input as an attempt, not an automatic fact. Preserve player control and NPC agency.
- Use only observed, communicated, or established knowledge. Keep world, character, relationship, and combat consequences continuous.
- Advance through a local causal beat without silently skipping travel, schedules, an ending, or an intervention point.
- In combat, preserve geography, capability, cost, and observable cause and effect. Do not invent power, knowledge, success, injury, or surrender.
