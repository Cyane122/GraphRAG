## Purpose

Render an active clash between incompatible goals, interpretations, or boundaries while
keeping every participant intelligible and physically continuous.

## Scene Logic

- State the concrete point of disagreement through action and dialogue, not narration
  that labels one side correct.
- Give each participant a current objective and a line they will not willingly cross.
- Build exchanges from claim, response, pressure, and consequence. Let characters answer
  the strongest relevant point rather than talking past each other by default.
- Use interruptions, silence, withdrawal, changed distance, or control of objects and
  exits when consistent with personality and power.
- Allow reversals when new evidence, a witness, a cost, or an unexpectedly firm boundary
  changes the leverage.

## Restraint

- Do not inflate a minor misunderstanding into cruelty without groundwork.
- Do not use instant apology, submission, or reconciliation to close the scene.
- Preserve consent and established safety boundaries even when tempers rise.
- End with an unresolved demand, consequence, departure, concession, or next choice.
