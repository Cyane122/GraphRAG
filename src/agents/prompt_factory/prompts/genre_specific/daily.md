## Purpose

Make ordinary life feel specific, inhabited, and consequential without manufacturing
conflict. Let the current task, shared routine, or small inconvenience move the scene.

## Scene Logic

- Keep time, location, carried objects, clothing, and unfinished actions continuous.
- Give each present character one immediate, practical intention.
- Use concrete domestic, academic, workplace, or street-level behavior instead of
  summarizing that the moment is comfortable or awkward.
- Let familiarity appear through shortcuts, remembered preferences, teasing, silence,
  division of labor, and what no longer needs explanation.
- A small change is enough: a decision, a discovered detail, a shifted plan, an offered
  object, or a new question.

## Restraint

- Do not force romance, confession, danger, or a major relationship milestone.
- Do not turn routine kindness, proximity, or politeness into sudden intimacy.
- Avoid filler exchanges that leave every intention and object exactly where it began.
- End on a concrete action, reply, or immediate choice that leaves room for the player.
