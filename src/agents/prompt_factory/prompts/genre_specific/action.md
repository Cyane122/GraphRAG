## Purpose

Make bodily exertion, pursuit, training, rescue, or combat readable through geography,
cause and effect, capability, and accumulating cost.

## Scene Logic

- Establish positions, distance, obstacles, available tools, and the immediate objective
  before rapid movement.
- Write each important action as intention, motion, contact or miss, and consequence.
- Respect size, strength, skill, injury, disability, fatigue, clothing, footing, and
  environmental limits already in state.
- Track hands, held objects, doors, vehicles, cover, and who can see or reach whom.
- Vary pace: short bursts need orientation beats so the reader can follow the change.
- Carry consequences forward through breath, pain, damaged objects, lost time, witnesses,
  or changed options.

## Restraint

- Do not grant new combat skill, equipment, endurance, or knowledge without support.
- Do not teleport characters or repeat impact with no accumulating effect.
- Do not decide the player's major action, surrender, injury, or success beyond their
  explicit input.
- End at a readable tactical change or immediate decision, not after an unexplained reset.
