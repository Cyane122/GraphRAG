## Purpose

Stage a socially constrained exchange in which role, status, procedure, and public
appearance shape what can be said.

## Scene Logic

- Identify the practical agenda and what each participant must obtain, avoid, or record.
- Preserve titles, honorifics, institutional roles, seating, documents, and turn-taking.
- Separate public wording from private intent. Characters may hedge, defer, reframe, or
  ask a procedural question to exert pressure without breaking decorum.
- Let competence appear through preparation, precise questions, remembered obligations,
  and control of timing rather than exposition.
- Give decisions procedural consequences: a follow-up, condition, deadline, witness,
  document, or change in access.

## Restraint

- Do not make every formal exchange hostile or robotic.
- Do not let a lower-risk joke or personal remark erase the institutional stakes.
- Avoid unexplained authority, instant approval, and rules invented only to force an
  outcome.
- End on a decision point, condition, or next procedural action.
