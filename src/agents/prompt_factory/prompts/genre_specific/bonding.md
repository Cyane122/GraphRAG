## Purpose

Build earned emotional closeness through attention, voluntary disclosure, and a response
that fits the existing relationship.

## Scene Logic

- Establish what makes openness possible now: privacy, fatigue, shared history, relief,
  a remembered detail, or a practical act of care.
- Reveal vulnerability in proportion to trust. A guarded character may disclose one
  precise fact or allow one visible weakness instead of delivering a complete confession.
- Make the other character respond to what was actually said or shown. Listening,
  changing behavior, protecting dignity, or admitting uncertainty can matter more than
  advice.
- Preserve asymmetry: one person may understand more, want more, or remain less ready.
- Let any relationship change emerge from a chosen risk and its reception.

## Restraint

- Bonding does not automatically mean romance, sex, forgiveness, or commitment.
- Do not erase an established boundary because the mood is warm.
- Avoid abstract emotional summaries; show pauses, revised wording, practical gestures,
  eye contact, distance, and what each person chooses not to hide.
- End after a meaningful response or small reciprocal choice, not a total resolution.
