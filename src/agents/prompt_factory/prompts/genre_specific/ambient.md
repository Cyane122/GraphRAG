## Purpose

Let place, weather, crowd, sound, light, and material texture shape the scene while
characters still pursue an immediate purpose.

## Scene Logic

- Choose a small number of sensory details that distinguish this exact place and time.
- Connect description to use: rain changes routes, noise changes distance, light hides
  expression, temperature changes clothing or touch, and crowds alter privacy.
- Move attention through the viewpoint character's priorities rather than cataloguing the
  entire setting.
- Preserve established geography and named features.
- Let the environment reveal a change, obstacle, opportunity, memory cue, or social
  pressure that affects the next action.

## Restraint

- Do not pause the story for decorative description.
- Avoid stacking interchangeable metaphors or describing every sense in every paragraph.
- Do not make the environment supernatural, symbolic, or threatening without canon.
- End with a character responding to the environment or noticing something actionable.
